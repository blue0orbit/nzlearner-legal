Product information

Using the app

These notes explain the current product. They are not final approved contractual terms.

Learning support

NZ Learner Licence Test is an independent preparation app for New Zealand Class 1 car learners. It does not issue licences, book tests or guarantee a pass. The provider questions are not claimed to be the complete official NZTA examination bank. Read the current official Road Code and follow the rules that apply when you drive.

Access and exams

Accounts are mandatory. The product provides one free mock exam daily on an installation, rewarded-ad extras when available, and unlimited exams for paid users. Exam assistance remains available; assisted attempts are labelled and excluded from readiness evidence.

Connectivity and local storage

Questions and images are bundled for offline study. Authentication, purchases and advertising may require connectivity. Progress, bookmarks and daily allowances are local to the installation, without cross-device synchronisation or enforcement. Purchase verification is client-side.

Paid features

Approved prices and the distinct benefits of Premium and Premium Plus are not yet available. No paid offer is made on this preview website. Google Play handles in-app payment; account deletion does not automatically refund a transaction.

Privacy and support

Read privacy and your choices and account deletion. Contact blue0orbit@gmail.com for assistance. The app name is used as the public contact identity.
