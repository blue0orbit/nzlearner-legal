Privacy and your choices

This preview describes the current NZ Learner Licence Test implementation. Contact blue0orbit@gmail.com about privacy or deletion.

What the app uses

PurposeInformation and controls
Your accountFirebase Authentication handles sign-in, including account identifiers, email and information supplied by the sign-in provider. An account is required. The NZ app uses a separate Firebase project.
LearningAnswers, results, bookmarks, progress and daily allowances are stored on your installation. Study questions and images are bundled offline. Learning progress does not synchronise to other devices.
PurchasesGoogle Play handles payment and purchase records. The app uses purchase state and tokens to acknowledge purchases and cache access for the signed-in account. It does not receive your full payment-card details. Purchase checks are client-side.
Advertising and consentUnity Ads provides advertising. Google UMP is included for advertising consent where required. Advertising providers may process device, network and advertising identifiers under their applicable settings. Final provider configuration and data declarations remain under review.
Optional usage analyticsGoogle Analytics for Firebase is off by default. If you opt in, the app sends fixed feature-event names without your answers, scores, account ID or purchase tokens. The SDK may also process device/app metadata and automatic events. Advertising-ID collection and advertising consent are disabled for Analytics.
Optional crash reportingFirebase Crashlytics is off by default. If you opt in, it receives crash information and device/app metadata, including device model, operating system, orientation, available memory and storage, app version and event time. The app removes exception messages and nested details from its reports and does not attach account IDs, credentials, purchase tokens or question answers. Controlled test reports have been verified. Broader third-party SDK behaviour and privacy review remain outstanding.
Other sign-in providersGoogle sign-in is included. Facebook Login code is also present, with automatic event logging disabled; its NZ configuration is not yet accepted for release. A provider handles information under its own terms when used.
Support and this websiteIf you email support, your email address, message and attachments are processed to respond. Do not send passwords or private keys. These static pages contain no tracking scripts or forms. The hosting provider may keep technical request logs.

Change your choices

In the app, open Profile → Usage and crash reporting. Usage analytics and crash reporting are separate choices. You can decline or withdraw either choice. This stops future collection through the app's controls; it does not erase reports already uploaded. Advertising choices are handled separately.

Deletion and retention

Delete your account or request help outside the app. Successful in-app deletion removes the Firebase account and clears account-owned learning data from that installation. An interrupted request may require signing in again and retrying. Deleting an account does not remotely erase another device's local data or automatically delete every provider's diagnostic, advertising, billing or support record.

Support and privacy-request emails are scheduled for deletion 90 days after resolution, except records needed for an ongoing dispute or legal obligation. The developer personally handles requests at the contact email above.

Verified Analytics settings retain event data for 2 months and user data for 14 months, with the user period renewed by new activity. These settings do not limit retention of standard aggregated reports. Google states that Crashlytics keeps crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. Firebase Authentication account data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days after customer-initiated deletion; logged IP addresses are kept for a few weeks.

The process for requesting removal of previously uploaded diagnostics and other provider records remains under review. No unverified deletion deadline is promised. Google Play may retain transaction records under its own policies.
