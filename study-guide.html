YOUR STUDY COMPANION

How to practise for your NZ learner licence.

Build understanding first. Use questions to check it. Let your mistakes tell you what to learn next.

This is a study routine, not official test instructions. For current requirements and road rules, use the NZTA Road Code.

1. Start with the Road Code

Read a section before you practise its topic. Pause at unfamiliar signs or situations and explain the rule in your own words. Understanding why a rule exists is more useful than memorising the position of an answer.

2. Keep practice sessions focused

Choose one topic at a time. Read the entire question and every option before answering. If a diagram is involved, look carefully at road markings, signs and the position of each vehicle.

3. Review answers, including correct guesses

A correct guess can hide a gap in understanding. Read the explanation, compare it with the official guidance and bookmark anything you cannot yet explain confidently.

4. Return after a break

Revisit difficult questions in a later session. Mix earlier topics with newer ones so that familiar answers do not replace understanding. A small, repeatable routine is easier to maintain than occasional long sessions.

5. Separate learning from checking readiness

Use assistance when you are learning. When you want to assess your own understanding, take an unassisted mock attempt, then review it afterwards. NZ Learner labels assisted attempts and excludes them from readiness evidence.

6. Prepare for the official appointment

Check current booking, identity and test-day requirements directly with NZTA and your chosen licensing agent. Do not rely on a practice app to confirm your eligibility or book your test.
