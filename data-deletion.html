Delete your account

Start account deletion in the app, or email a request without reinstalling it.

Request deletion by email

Send an email to blue0orbit@gmail.com with the subject “NZ Learner Licence Test account deletion”. Send it from the email associated with your app account, where possible, and state that you want your app account and associated data deleted.

Start a deletion request

If your email application does not open, copy the address above into your email service. Do not send your password, sign-in code, purchase token, private key or identity document. If you cannot access the account email, explain that in your message. A request needs ownership verification before account removal.

This support process is being prepared for release. Mailbox monitoring, the verification procedure and response times still need owner confirmation. Sending this email does not itself delete an account.

Delete inside the app

  1. Connect to the internet and sign in to the account you intend to delete.
  2. Open Profile and select Delete account.
  3. Read the confirmation and confirm deletion. Follow any request to sign in again, then retry deletion.
  4. Wait for the result. If the operation is interrupted or its outcome is uncertain, reopen the app and follow the retry instructions or contact support.

What is removed

Successful in-app deletion removes your NZ Firebase Authentication account and clears that account's local learning data, saved results, bookmarks and related access/allowance records on the installation. Local cleanup is designed to resume after interruption and is restricted to the account being deleted. Local cleanup alone is not proof that the remote account was removed.

Other devices and service records

Progress is local to each installation, so a web request cannot erase local learning data on a different phone. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it on those devices to remove remaining local data. Uninstalling alone does not delete the Firebase account.

Account deletion does not automatically erase previously submitted Analytics or Crashlytics records, advertising-provider records, support emails or Google Play transaction records. Provider retention settings and any additional deletion steps remain under review. A deletion request does not itself issue a purchase refund.

See privacy and your choices for the current data inventory and outstanding retention information.
