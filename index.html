GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

Can I download NZ Learner now?

Not yet. The Android app is being tested and prepared for release. This site describes the current build and planned pricing. A verified Google Play link will be added when it is available.

Is this the official NZTA learner test app?

No. NZ Learner is an independent study app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NZTA. Its 366-question bank is not claimed to be the complete official examination bank. Study the official NZTA Road Code alongside your practice.

Will questions and images work offline?

Yes, the installed app bundles its study questions, translations and images. Accounts are mandatory, and authentication, purchases and advertising may need an internet connection.

Does my progress sync between phones?

No. Progress, bookmarks and daily exam allowances are local to each installation. Clearing storage or uninstalling can remove local learning records. Purchase checking is client-side; there is no server-verified billing or cross-device quota enforcement.

What does the Pro upgrade cost?

The approved planned price is NZ$5.99 as a single, one-time purchase. It is not a subscription. The Play product is not available for purchase yet.

How can I contact you or delete my account?

Email blue0orbit@gmail.com. Visit support for help or follow the account-deletion instructions, including requests made without the app.
