Clear expectations

An account is required. Learning progress and daily exam allowances are stored on each installation; they do not synchronise across devices. Purchase entitlement is checked in the app with Google Play, without server verification.

Questions and answers are in English. Seven additional interface-language choices remain available. Their translation drafts are complete, but native-language review is still pending. These interface translations do not translate the question bank.

This is an independent study app. The provider question bank is not represented as the complete official NZTA examination bank. Content and visual review are ongoing. Use the current official Road Code alongside your study.
