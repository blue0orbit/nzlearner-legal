What is in the current build?

Study without content downloads

366 practice questions, 12 categories and 203 images are bundled in the app. English plus ten additional languages cover questions, answers, explanations and the interface.

A choice of practice tools

Practise by topic, search questions, save bookmarks, review your progress and try mock exams. Assisted attempts are labelled and excluded from readiness evidence.

Free practice and a one-time Pro option

The planned free option includes one mock exam per day on each installation, with rewarded-ad extras when available. The planned Pro upgrade is NZ$5.99 once, with unlimited mocks, no ads, scenarios without the ad gate and full progress insights. Purchases are not available yet.

Before you install

  • An account is required. Authentication, purchases and ads may require internet access.
  • Progress, bookmarks and daily exam allowances stay local to each installation.
  • Purchases are checked in the app; cross-device quota enforcement and server-verified billing are not provided.
  • NZ Learner is independent of NZTA and does not claim to contain the complete official examination bank.

For account or privacy questions, visit support, privacy information or account deletion.
