Support

How can we help?

Contact NZ Learner Licence Test at blue0orbit@gmail.com.

Email support

Reporting a problem

Describe what happened and the steps you took. Include your app version and Android version if available. Avoid including passwords, sign-in codes, purchase tokens or private keys. Check screenshots for personal information before sending them.

Accounts and deletion

An account is required. Sign-in and account deletion may need an internet connection. You can request account deletion without the app.

Offline study and saved progress

Questions and images are included after installation. Progress and the daily free exam allowance belong to that installation; there is no cross-device synchronisation. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove local progress.

Purchases and rewarded exams

Purchase restoration needs Google Play connectivity and the appropriate Play and app accounts. A pending or failed purchase is not a completed purchase. Rewarded extras depend on an available ad and its completion. The single planned Pro upgrade costs NZ$5.99 once, with unlimited mocks, no ads, scenarios without the ad gate and full progress insights. It is not available to purchase yet.

Question content and languages

The current build includes English and ten additional languages for the interface, questions, answers and explanations. Coverage is complete; independent language and content review is still in progress. To report a possible content error, describe the question and why it may be incorrect. Content review is ongoing; the app does not claim to contain the complete official NZTA examination bank.

Support response times have not yet been confirmed. This app does not provide emergency services.
